# PageToPDF privacy policy

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

PageToPDF is a free, open-source Chrome extension that captures web pages as PDF or image files. It is built to work entirely on your device.

## What PageToPDF does not do

- It does not collect, store or transmit any personal data, browsing history, page content or usage statistics.
- It does not send anything to any server. The extension makes no network requests of its own and contains no analytics, ads or tracking.
- It does not sell or share data with anyone.

## What stays on your device

- **Captures.** When you capture a page, the screenshots and the text and link positions used for the searchable PDF are stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB), so you can reopen, annotate and re-export them. You can delete any capture from the viewer or the "All captures" page, and removing the extension deletes all of them.
- **Settings.** Your preferences (format, page size, header and footer options and so on) are kept in Chrome's extension storage. If you have Chrome Sync turned on, Chrome syncs these settings between your own devices. PageToPDF never sees or receives them anywhere else.
- **Exported files.** PDF, PNG and JPG files are saved to your computer through Chrome's downloads.

Captures taken in an incognito window are kept only for that incognito session and are deleted when it ends.

## Permissions

PageToPDF only accesses a page when you start a capture on it (toolbar button, right-click menu or keyboard shortcut). The optional "all sites" access and the high-resolution mode, which uses Chrome's debugger API on the tab being captured, are used only to take the screenshots and are released as soon as the capture ends.
